RAG System User Guide

1. Upload Documents

Click the Attach file button or drag & drop documents. Supported formats: .pdf, .csv, .xlsx, .txt.

2. Automatic Vector Embeddings

Document text is chunked and embedded via all-MiniLM-L6-v2 into a local FAISS vector database.

3. Switch Intelligence Model

Select your preferred AI engine from the topbar: Google Gemini 1.5, ChatGPT (GPT-4o), or offline Llama 3.2 3B (Local).

4. Multi-Language Answers

Click the Language icon at the bottom right to receive answers in English, Hinglish, or Pure Hindi.

5. Intelligence Hub & Sources

Toggle the Insights button in the topbar to view citations, confidence scores, and dataset statistics.
